How it works

  1. Upload an image — drag & drop or click to browse.
  2. Resize — set a width/height, use a quick percentage, and lock the aspect ratio.
  3. Format — pick PNG, JPEG or WEBP and adjust the quality for compression.
  4. Pixel-perfect — use crisp nearest-neighbour scaling when enlarging pixel art.
  5. Export — download the resized image at your chosen size.
