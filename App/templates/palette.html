How it works

  1. Upload an image — drag & drop or click to browse.
  2. Colours — choose how many colours to extract (2–32).
  3. Sort — order swatches by how common they are or by hue.
  4. Swatches — click any swatch to copy its hex code.
  5. Export — copy all hex codes, copy CSS variables, or download as JSON / PNG.
