Turn photos into cross-stitch patterns

Stitchee is a free, browser-based cross-stitch pattern maker. Upload a photo and it is pixelated into a grid of yarn colours, with every stitch rendered as a woven cross on a virtual canvas. Pick your own palette, paint by hand, or start from a blank canvas.

When you are happy with your design, Stitchee builds a yarn legend that shows exactly which colours you need and how many stitches of each, then exports it as a crisp PNG chart you can print or share. Everything runs in your browser, so your projects stay private and there is nothing to install.

Photo to pattern

Drop in any image and Stitchee pixelates it and matches every cell to the nearest yarn colour in your palette.

Yarn colour matching

Choose from a rich palette of real yarn colours, or filter to find the exact shade you have in your stash.

Printable legend

Get an automatic yarn legend with the exact colours and stitch counts you need for every part of your design.

Export and save

Download your chart as a high-resolution PNG, save your project as JSON, and come back to it later — all in your browser.

How it works

  1. Choose a canvas size — up to 500×500 stitches.
  2. Image tab — upload a picture; it pixelates and matches colours.
  3. Trace overlay — lay a photo over the chart, fade it with the opacity slider, then switch it off to see your stitches. Use the Align tool to move it and stretch it by its corner and edge points until the cells line up.
  4. Blank tab — start empty and paint by hand.
  5. Custom mode — pickc a colour and click a cell to stitch it; right-click to clear it.
  6. Navigate — middle-click drag pans the chart, Ctrl + scroll zooms it, and clicking any sidebar heading collapses that panel.
  7. Legend — shows exactly which yarns and stitch count of each.
  8. Export / Save — download a PNG or JSON, or save it on the server.
